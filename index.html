Category

Arcade

Fast-paced, skill-based games. Focus on reflexes, timing, and quick decision-making. Examples: Pac-Man, Space Invaders, Fruit Ninja, Subway Surfers.

Puzzle

Logic and problem-solving oriented. Usually not time-based but can be. Examples: Tetris, Candy Crush, Sudoku, Monument Valley.

Simulation

Games that mimic real-world activities or systems. Players manage, build, or simulate experiences. Examples: The Sims, Flight Simulator, Farming Simulator, RollerCoaster Tycoon.
